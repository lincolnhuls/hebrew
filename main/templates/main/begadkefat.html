Begadkefat Letters

6 letters in Hebrew have two types of pronunciation. These letters are often called "begadkefat" letters, coming from thier name. The 6 letters are Bet, Gimel, Dalet, Kaf, Pe, and Tav. These changes in pronunciation are marked by what is known as a "dagesh", a small dot that is added to the letter. When you see a letter with the dagesh, it is the hard pronunciation. Without the dagesh, it is the soft pronunciation. These letters also have two names, depending on if they have their dagesh or not. When refering to the letters, use the name from the first lesson, the second name used to describe the dagesh is used less often.
