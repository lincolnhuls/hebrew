Lesson 4: Aspect — Learn

Perfect vs. Imperfect

Important to understanding Biblical Hebrew is understanding the aspect of verbs as you translate. Verbs in Biblical Hebrew hold much more information than just the action. Depending on the aspect, the verb will also tell you who is preforming the action. For example, אמֶר is the Hebrew verb meaning "to speak". וַיֹּאמֶר Is the imperfect 3MS aspect of the verb. This adds to the verb, taking it from just "to say", to "he said, or he says". When looking a verb up in a lexicon, the vowels are very important to getting the right entry.
