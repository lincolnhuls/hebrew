Lesson 7: Root Restoration — Learn

Finding the third root letter

Remember that there are always three root letters for a verb. There are a limited number of letters that can fall off of a Hebrew verb root. Until you become more familiar with Hebrew, a practical approach to finding the missing letter would be trial and error. For that purpose, here is a list of letters that could fall off of a verb and where they fall off.

The underscores represent the two consonants of the verb that remain after removing conjunctions, prepositions, and pronouns. Try one of the following and then consult the lexicon.

If the first letter of the vrb fell off, it could be a He or Vav, if the middle letter fell off, it could be a Yod or Vav, and if the last letter fell off, it could be a Yod, or Nun, with He or Lamed only occuring with specific verbs.
